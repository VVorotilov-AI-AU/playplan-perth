# Privacy

PlayPlan Perth is designed to work without identifying a family.

It does not ask for or collect names, children’s names, birthdays, accounts, home addresses or contact details. Temporary GPS access is requested only after “Use my location” is tapped. Coordinates are used for that search and are not written to browser storage.

Favorites, visit tags, optional 280-character notes and non-GPS search preferences remain in local browser storage on the current device. Clearing site data removes them. No analytics or advertising trackers are included.

Weather requests send approximate search coordinates to Open-Meteo. Map tiles are requested from the configured OpenStreetMap tile provider while the map is visible. Google Maps opens only when the user chooses its link.
